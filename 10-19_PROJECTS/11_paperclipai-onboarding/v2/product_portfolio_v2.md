# Product Portfolio for Digital Project Packs

## 1. Portfolio Overview
KG Services offers 38-39 digital project packs across 4 phases, curated from 197 master projects identified in Project 11 and Project 12.
Each pack is a bundle of templates and tools designed for SMMEs to run specific business projects without starting from scratch.

## 2. Pack Structure
Each project pack consists of 9 files (delivered as ZIP download) plus a Systeme.io course containing video lessons:
  1. 01-Introduction.docx
  2. 02-Proposal.docx
  3. 03-Checklist.xlsx (REQUIRED in every pack)
  4. 04-User-Guide.docx
  5. 06-AI-Prompt.txt (done UPFRONT — user inputs parameters in their LLM)
  6. 07-Core-Deliverable (varies by project)
  7. 08-Supporting-Asset
  8. 09-Reference-Asset
  9. 10-Video-Script.docx (numbered LAST — internal for video production, used to create Systeme.io course)

Note: File 05 is intentionally omitted; the sequence jumps from 04 to 06.

## 3. File Descriptions
- **01-Introduction.docx**: Overview of the project, objectives, and expected outcomes.
- **02-Proposal.docx**: Template for proposing the project to stakeholders or clients.
- **03-Checklist.xlsx**: Step-by-step checklist to guide project execution (mandatory).
- **04-User-Guide.docx**: Detailed instructions on how to use the pack and customize templates.
- **06-AI-Prompt.txt**: Pre-engineered AI prompts that the user runs in their LLM to generate customized starting points based on their parameters.
- **07-Core-Deliverable**: The main output of the project (varies by pack, e.g., business plan document, marketing plan, financial model).
- **08-Supporting-Asset**: Supplementary materials (e.g., slide deck, budget template, sample report).
- **09-Reference-Asset**: Reference materials, examples, or further reading.
- **10-Video-Script.docx**: Internal script used to produce video lessons for the Systeme.io course (not included in customer ZIP).

## 4. Delivery Format
- Customer receives a ZIP file containing files 01-04, 06-09 (9 files) upon purchase.
- Access to Systeme.io course is granted, which includes video lessons derived from file 10.
- No physical shipment; all digital delivery via Systeme.io.

## 5. Pricing Tiers
- **Single Project**: $10 (one-time) – one pack of the customer’s choice.
- **Outcome Pack**: $50 (one-time) – bundle of multiple packs (typically 5-10 packs focused on an outcome, e.g., "Business Launch Pack").
- **Complete System**: $TBD (one-time) – access to all 38-39 packs (to be offered after full library is created).
- No subscriptions, no recurring fees.

## 6. Phases (per Project 11 curation)
Packs are organized into 4 phases (details to be defined in Phase documentation):
- Phase 1: Foundation packs (e.g., business plan, marketing plan, financial plan)
- Phase 2: Growth packs (e.g., sales plan, hiring plan, operations plan)
- Phase 3: Optimization packs (e.g., process improvement, customer retention, digital transformation)
- Phase 4: Innovation packs (e.g., new product launch, market expansion, AI integration)

## 7. Creation Process
- Packs are researched and curated by agents + CEO.
- Templates are created using HTML/CSS for A4 print-ready worksheets (agent-generated).
- AI prompts are engineered upfront for each pack.
- Video lessons are produced from internal scripts (file 10) using OBS Studio and DaVinci Resolve (free).
- Packs are assembled into ZIP and uploaded to Systeme.io.

## 8. Piracy Protection
- The Systeme.io course delivery method ensures content is consumed in sequence and is not easily shared as raw files.
- Video lessons are only accessible via logged-in Systeme.io account.
- ZIP files are provided, but the course adds value and discourages redistribution.
