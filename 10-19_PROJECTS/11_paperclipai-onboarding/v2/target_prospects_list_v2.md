# Target Prospects List for Digital Project Packs

## 1. Overview
This document defines the target customer profile for KG Services Digital Project Packs.
All pricing is accessible ($10/$50 one-time) to enable global adoption by startups and small businesses.

## 2. Primary Target Customers
- **Global SMMEs** (Solopreneurs, Small Businesses, Micro Enterprises)
- Any size, any country, any industry that runs business projects
- No revenue filters — accessible pricing for starting businesses worldwide
- Examples: freelancers, consultants, local shops, online businesses, startups

## 3. Secondary Target Customers
- Founders launching new ventures who need ready-to-use templates
- Consultants who need professional packs for client engagements
- Agencies that need standardized deliverables for multiple clients
- These customers also benefit from the low cost and instant access

## 4. Pain Point
**“I need to run this project but don't have time to create documents from scratch.”**
- Customers know what they want to accomplish but lack the time or expertise to build professional templates
- They seek a trusted, affordable shortcut to get started immediately

## 5. Sourcing Channels
- **Primary:** Facebook groups (global, free, easy) → Systeme.io funnel
  - Search for groups: “small business owners”, “entrepreneurs”, “freelancers”, “startup founders”, industry-specific groups
  - Post value: free project template (lead magnet) → capture email → nurture to $10 tripwire
- **Secondary:** 
  - LinkedIn groups (professional networks, industry forums)
  - Reddit communities (r/smallbusiness, r/entrepreneur, r/freelance, industry subreddits)
  - Global forums (Warrior Forum, Indie Hackers, specialized BBS)
- All channels are organic; no paid acquisition for v1

## 6. Outreach Approach
- No cold email, no spam, no paid ads in v1
- Provide genuine value in groups: answer questions, share expertise, offer free templates
- Build trust and authority before presenting the offer
- Funnel: Facebook post → free lead magnet (single project) → $10 tripwire → $50 outcome pack → Complete System (later)

## 7. Qualification Criteria
- Any business owner or professional who runs projects and needs templates
- Interest shown by downloading free lead magnet or engaging with content
- No minimum revenue, employee count, or tech stack requirements
- Willingness to pay $10 for a immediate solution is the key qualifier

## 8. Expansion Strategy
- Start globally via Facebook groups (lowest barrier, widest reach)
- As packs are created, create niche-specific lead magnets (e.g., “free restaurant business plan template”)
- Use Systeme.io automation to segment and nurture based on lead magnet topic
- Reinvest early revenue into creating more packs and refining funnel
