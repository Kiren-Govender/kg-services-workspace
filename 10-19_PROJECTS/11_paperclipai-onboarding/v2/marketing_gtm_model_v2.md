# Marketing & Go-to-Market Model for Digital Project Packs

## 1. Marketing Motion Overview
KG Services employs a marketing-led model using Facebook groups and Systeme.io funnels to attract SMMEs globally, offering free lead magnets and low-cost project packs.

## 2. Target Audience & Segmentation
- Primary: Global SMMEs (solopreneurs, small businesses, micro-enterprises) needing ready-to-use project templates
- Secondary: Founders, consultants, agencies needing ready-to-use templates
- Segmentation by project type (e.g., business plan, marketing plan, financial plan, etc.)

## 3. Demand Generation Tactics
### 3.1 Facebook Groups
- Post free project templates in relevant Facebook groups (global, free, easy)
- Engage group members, answer questions, build trust
- Direct interested users to Systeme.io landing page for free lead magnet

### 3.2 Lead Magnets & Funnel
- Free single project ($10 value) as lead magnet → captured email
- Automated email sequence: upsell to outcome pack ($50)
- Outcome pack → upsell to Complete System (later)
- No inside sales, no ABM, no partner channels, no subscription tiers

### 3.3 Content Strategy
- Blog posts targeting project-type keywords globally (e.g., "how to write a business plan", "marketing checklist")
- Repurpose blog content for Facebook posts and email sequences
- Evidence collection: track downloads, collect user benefits and reviews for social proof

## 4. Pricing Strategy
- Free lead magnet (single project template) → email capture
- Tripwire: $10 single project (one-time)
- Core offer: $50 outcome pack (multiple projects, one-time)
- Later: $Complete System (all packs, one-time) after library built
- No subscription tiers, no freemium SaaS pricing

## 5. Promotional Strategy
- Social proof: display collected reviews/benefits on landing pages
- Referral: existing customers get discount for sharing
- Limited-time bonuses for early adopters (extra packs)

## 6. Channel Strategy
### 6.1 Facebook Groups (Primary)
- Organic posting in global SMME groups, founder groups, consultant networks
- No paid ads initially (free reach)

### 6.2 Systeme.io Funnel (Secondary)
- Landing pages, email automation, course delivery
- Affiliate system via Systeme.io (later)

### 6.3 Global Communities
- LinkedIn groups, Reddit forums, global forums as secondary sources

## 7. Metrics & ROI Measurement
### 7.1 Leading Indicators
- Facebook group post engagement (likes, comments)
- Click-through to landing page
- Lead magnet conversion rate (visitors → email)
- Tripwire conversion rate (leads → $10 customers)
- Outcome pack conversion rate ($10 → $50)

### 7.2 Lagging Indicators
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) – target very low (near zero)
- Conversion funnel velocity
- Revenue per visitor
- Social proof volume (reviews, testimonials)

### 7.3 Reporting
- Weekly Facebook group activity report
- Weekly Systeme.io funnel dashboard
- Monthly revenue and customer count
- Quarterly social proof compilation

## 8. Budget Allocation Guidelines
- 0% paid ads (rely on organic Facebook groups)
- 0% marketing personnel (agents + CEO only)
- 0% agency retainers
- 100% time invested by agents + CEO in content creation and funnel optimization
- Review and adjust weekly based on Facebook group response

## 9. Sales & Marketing Alignment
- No separate sales team; marketing fulfills entire funnel via Systeme.io
- Weekly sync between agents creating content and CEO overseeing funnel
- Shared Systeme.io analytics as closed-loop reporting
- Joint planning of content calendar and funnel updates

*This model serves as the foundation for executing marketing-led growth for Digital Project Packs via Systeme.io.*
