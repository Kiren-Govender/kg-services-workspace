# Financial Model for Digital Project Packs (Reach-First v1)

## Revenue Assumptions
- **Product Type**: Digital project packs (one-time purchase via Systeme.io)
- **Pricing Tiers**:
  - **Single Project**: $10 (one-time) – one pack
  - **Outcome Pack**: $50 (one-time) – bundle of multiple packs (e.g., 5-10 packs)
  - **Complete System**: $TBD (one-time) – full library (after all packs created, v2)
- **NO recurring subscriptions** – all one-time purchases

## Cost Structure (Near-Zero)
### Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) - Digital Delivery
- Systeme.io platform fee: $27/month (unlimited plan) – **fixed cost**
- Transaction fees (payment processors): ~5% + $0.30 per transaction (average)
- Average transaction value: ~$15 (weighted between $10 and $50)
- Estimated COGS per sale: $1.00-$1.50
- **COGS % of Revenue**: ~5-10% (very low for digital products)

### Operating Expenses (Agent-First, v1)
1. **Content Creation**: 
   - **Agents + CEO only** – no external hiring.
   - Time invested by agents/CEO is sunk cost (not counted as cash outflow).
   - **No cost** for content creation in v1 (agent time covered by Hermes subscription).

2. **Design & Formatting**:
   - **Agent-generated HTML/CSS** via `high-end-visual-design` skill – free.
   - **NO designer cost** in v1.

3. **Marketing**:
   - **$0 advertising budget** – rely on organic Facebook groups.
   - **NO paid ads, NO content distribution cost**.

4. **Platform & Tools**:
   - Systeme.io: $27/month (unlimited) – **only recurring cost**.
   - Notion: Free plan sufficient for knowledge base.
   - OBS Studio, DaVinci Resolve: Free.
   - Hermes agent: Included in subscription.
   - **Miscellaneous**: $0.

5. **Customer Support**:
   - Handled by agents/CEO via Systeme.io email and Notion KB – **no additional cost**.

### Total Monthly Cost (v1)
- Fixed: Systeme.io $27/month
- Variable: Transaction fees ~$1.00 per sale
- Example Month 6: 225 sales → $27 + $225 = ~$252
- **Costs are minimal and scale linearly with sales**.

## Profitability Metrics
- **Gross Margin**: 90-95% (after transaction fees)
- **Contribution Margin**: ~85-90% (after minimal variable costs)
- **Break-even Analysis**:
  - Fixed costs: $27/month (Systeme.io)
  - Variable cost per sale: ~$1.00
  - Break-even volume: **<20 sales/month** at $10 average price (actually ~13 sales/month to cover $27 fixed)
  - With mix of $10 and $50 packs, break-even is even lower.
- **Extremely low break-even** – achievable quickly with organic reach.

## Cash Flow Considerations
- **Upfront Investment**: $0 external – agent time only (covered by Hermes).
- **Revenue Recognition**: Immediate upon purchase via Systeme.io.
- **Payment Processing**: Weekly/monthly deposits from Systeme.io.
- **Working Capital**: Near zero – no inventory, minimal receivables.

## Key Financial Ratios (Target v1)
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: ~$0 (organic Facebook groups, agent time only)
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: ~$15 (mix of $10 and $50 packs)
- **Purchase Frequency**: One-time, with upsell from $10 to $50.
- **Refund Rate**: Target <1% (digital delivery, clear expectations, piracy-resistant).

## Sensitivity Analysis
- **Price Elasticity**: Low price ($10/$50) enables global adoption; volume-driven.
- **Volume Scaling**: As packs library grows, outcome packs become more attractive.
- **Seasonality**: Marketing via Facebook groups is less seasonal; adjust posting frequency.

## Investment Requirements (Initial)
- **$0 external investment** – all production by agents + CEO.
- **Tools**: Systeme.io subscription ($27/month) – operational expense, not upfront.
- **Content**: Generated by agents during Hermes runtime.
- **Marketing**: Organic posts by agents/CEO in Facebook groups.

## Long-Term Financial Outlook (v1 → v2)
- **Months 1-6**: Focus on creating initial packs, validating demand, collecting testimonials.
- **Months 6-12**: Library growth (~20-30 packs), refine funnel, prepare Outcome Packs.
- **After Month 12**: Introduce Complete System (v2) after full library, consider price increase based on evidence.
- **Revenue Growth**: Primarily from volume and increasing AOV as customers buy more packs and bundles.

---
*This financial model defines a reach-first, agent-first approach for v1 Digital Project Packs: near-zero costs, $0 external investment, break-even under 20 sales/month, and a clear path to v2 after validation.*
