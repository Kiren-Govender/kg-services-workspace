# KG Services Company Operating Manual
## Digital Project Packs Business Model

### Core Business Overview
KG Services creates and sells digital project packs/bundles for businesses via Systeme.io.
We do NOT build or sell SaaS automation platforms.

### Product Portfolio
- 197 master projects identified → curated to 38-39 projects across 4 phases
- Each pack = 9 deliverable files (ZIP package for customer):
  1. 01-Introduction.docx
  2. 02-Proposal.docx
  3. 03-Checklist.xlsx (REQUIRED in every pack)
  4. 04-User-Guide.docx
  5. 05-Video-Script.docx (internal only — used to produce video)
  6. 06-AI-Prompt.txt
  7. 07-Core-Deliverable (varies by project)
  8. 08-Supporting-Asset
  9. 09-Reference-Asset
- Video lessons: MP4 delivered via Systeme.io course module (post-purchase)

### Target Customers
- **Primary**: SMMEs (Small, Medium, Micro Enterprises) — South Africa initially
- **Secondary**: Founders, consultants, agencies who need ready-to-use project templates
- **Pain Point**: "I need to run this project/type of work but don't have time to create all the documents from scratch"
- **Value Proposition**: "Professional project packs ready to use — save weeks of document creation"

### Pricing Model
- **Individual packs**: $29-$99 each (one-time purchase)
- **Bundles/Phases**: $199-$499 (phase-based collections)
- **Full catalog access**: $997-$1,997 (lifetime or annual)
- **NO recurring subscriptions for core product**

### Go-to-Market Strategy (Marketing-Led via Systeme.io)
- **Funnel**: Systeme.io landing pages → email sequences → course delivery
- **Content**: Blog posts, case studies, free templates as lead magnets
- **Email**: Automated sequences in Systeme.io
- **NO inside sales team, NO ABM, NO partner channels (yet)**

### Delivery Model
- **Instant digital delivery** via Systeme.io (ZIP download + course access)
- **Support**: Email only (via Systeme.io), knowledge base in Notion
- **NO onboarding calls, NO implementation, NO SLAs, NO CSMs**

### Organizational Structure
- Content Creators (document/template creation)
- Designers (visual assets, formatting)
- Marketers (funnel optimization, email campaigns, content distribution)
- Customer Support (email-based, knowledge base management)
- **NO engineers, PMs, or CSMs for SaaS product**

### Key Operational Processes
1. Content Creation → Document/Template Development
2. Pack Assembly → ZIP packaging with 9 core files
3. Systeme.io Setup → Landing pages, email sequences, course modules
4. Marketing Execution → Blog, social, lead magnets
5. Sales Funnel → Automated via Systeme.io
6. Delivery & Support → Instant download + email support

### Success Metrics
- Conversion rates from landing pages to purchase
- Average order value (individual vs bundles)
- Customer satisfaction (post-purchase email feedback)
- Refund rate (<5% target)
- Content production velocity (packs/month)

### Compliance & Quality Standards
- All documents professionally formatted and branded
- Checklists validated for completeness
- Video scripts produced for all packs
- AI prompts tested and optimized
- Regular updates based on customer feedback
