# KG Services Hiring Plan
## For Digital Project Packs Business (Content/Product Team)

### Overview
We hire for content creation, design, and marketing roles — NOT for engineering, product management, or customer success roles associated with SaaS.

### Organizational Structure (Phase 1: Founder-Led)
- **Founder/CEO (Kiren)**: Sets product vision, approves packs, oversees marketing strategy
- **Content Creator(s)**: Research, write, and structure project pack documents
- **Designer(s)**: Format documents, create visual assets, design Systeme.io pages
- **Marketer(s)**: Set up funnels, write email sequences, manage traffic sources
- **Customer Support (Part-time)**: Handle email inquiries, maintain knowledge base
- **Optional**: Video Editor/AI Specialist (for video lessons and AI prompt optimization)

### Roles & Responsibilities

#### 1. Content Creator (Project Pack Author)
- **Primary Responsibility**: Research and write the 9 core files for each project pack
  - 01-Introduction.docx
  - 02-Proposal.docx
  - 03-Checklist.xlsx (MUST be accurate and actionable)
  - 04-User-Guide.docx
  - 05-Video-Script.docx (internal)
  - 06-AI-Prompt.txt
  - 07-Core-Deliverable (varies)
  - 08-Supporting-Asset
  - 09-Reference-Asset
- **Skills Required**:
  - Strong research and writing ability
  - Understanding of business processes and project management
  - Ability to create clear, actionable checklists and guides
  - Familiarity with Microsoft Office/Google Docs
  - Basic understanding of AI prompting (for AI-Prompt.txt)
- **Output**: 1 complete project pack every 1-2 weeks (depending on complexity)
- **Reporting To**: Founder/CEO or designated product lead

#### 2. Designer (Document & Visual Designer)
- **Primary Responsibility**:
  - Format all documents to brand standards (fonts, colors, headers)
  - Create visual assets (diagrams, charts, icons) for packs
  - Design Systeme.io landing pages and course layout
  - Ensure visual consistency across all deliverables
- **Skills Required**:
  - Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint (or Google equivalents)
  - Basic graphic design (Canva Pro or Adobe Suite)
  - Understanding of UI/UX for landing pages
  - Attention to detail
- **Output**: Designed documents and visual assets for 1-2 packs per week
- **Reporting To**: Founder/CEO or Content Lead

#### 3. Marketer (Funnel & Traffic Specialist)
- **Primary Responsibility**:
  - Set up and optimize Systeme.io funnels (landing pages, email sequences, course delivery)
  - Write and schedule email broadcast and automation sequences
  - Drive traffic via SEO blogging, social media, and lead magnets
  - Analyze funnel metrics and optimize for conversion
  - Manage affiliate/referral programs (future)
- **Skills Required**:
  - Proficiency with Systeme.io (funnels, email automation, course creation)
  - Copywriting and email marketing
  - Basic SEO and keyword research
  - Social media management (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest)
  - Analytics (Google Analytics, Systeme.io stats)
- **Output**: 1-2 new funnels per month, weekly email sequences, daily traffic generation
- **Reporting To**: Founder/CEO

#### 4. Customer Support Specialist (Part-time/As-Needed)
- **Primary Responsibility**:
  - Monitor and respond to customer emails via Systeme.io/helpdesk
  - Maintain and update knowledge base in Notion
  - Create FAQ documents and troubleshooting guides
  - Process refunds (if applicable) and handle basic issues
- **Skills Required**:
  - Excellent written communication
  - Empathy and problem-solving
  - Basic technical understanding (ZIP files, document access)
  - Familiarity with Notion and email ticketing systems
- **Output**: Timely responses (<24 hours), updated knowledge base
- **Reporting To**: Founder/CEO

#### 5. Video Editor/AI Specialist (Optional/Contract)
- **Primary Responsibility**:
  - Edit video lessons from scripts (05-Video-Script.docx) into MP4 format
  - Optimize AI prompts (06-AI-Prompt.txt) for best results
  - Create short promotional videos for marketing
- **Skills Required**:
  - Video editing (Camtasia, Adobe Premiere, or free alternatives)
  - Understanding of AI tools and prompt engineering
  - Basic audio cleanup and formatting
- **Output**: Video lessons for each pack, optimized AI prompts
- **Reporting To**: Content Creator or Founder

### Hiring Timeline & Phases

#### Phase 1: Founder-Led (Months 1-3)
- Founder creates first 5-10 packs to validate market
- Uses contractors/freelancers for design and marketing setup
- Focus: Product-market fit, refining pack template

#### Phase 2: Early Team (Months 4-6)
- Hire 1 part-time Content Creator (20 hrs/week)
- Hire 1 part-time Designer (10 hrs/week)
- Marketer role handled by founder or contractor
- Support handled by founder or VA
- Goal: Produce 2 packs per week, establish funnel

#### Phase 3: Scaling Team (Months 7-12)
- Content Creator: Full-time or 2 part-time
- Designer: Full-time or 2 part-time
- Marketer: Dedicated role (full-time or specialist)
- Support: Part-time dedicated
- Consider Video Editor if volume justifies
- Goal: 4+ packs per week, multiple bundles, optimized funnels

### Compensation Guidelines (South Africa Market)
- **Content Creator**: R150-R250/hour (freelance) or R15,000-R25,000/month (part-time)
- **Designer**: R180-R300/hour (freelance) or R12,000-R20,000/month (part-time)
- **Marketer**: R200-R350/hour (freelance) or R18,000-R30,000/month (part-time)
- **Support Specialist**: R100-R150/hour (freelance) or R8,000-R12,000/month (part-time)
- **Video Editor**: R200-R400/hour (project-based)

### Recruitment Sources
- Freelance platforms: Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer.co.za
- Local job boards: Careers24, PNet, Gumjobs
- Social media: LinkedIn, Facebook groups (SA freelancers, digital nomads)
- Referrals: Network of business coaches, consultants
- Internships: Recent graduates in business, English, design

### Onboarding & Training
1. **Business Model Training**: Deep dive into project packs vs SaaS
2. **Tools Training**: Systeme.io, Microsoft Office, Notion, Canva
3. **Pack Template Workshop**: Walk through an example pack
4. **Quality Standards**: Review checklist for each file type
5. **First Pack Collaboration**: New hire creates pack under supervision
6. **Ongoing Feedback**: Weekly review of output, metrics discussion

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
#### Content Creator
- Packs completed per month
- Quality score (peer review/checklist compliance)
- Time to first draft
- Revision cycles required

#### Designer
- Documents designed per week
- Brand consistency score
- Turnaround time for design requests
- Visual asset library growth

#### Marketer
- Funnel conversion rate (visitor → lead → customer)
- Email open/click rates
- Traffic volume and sources
- Cost per acquisition (if paid ads)
- Revenue generated per funnel

#### Support Specialist
- Response time to customer inquiries
- Customer satisfaction rating (post-support)
- Knowledge base articles created/updated
- Refund/request rate

### Culture & Values
- **Output-Oriented**: Focus on completed packs and customer results
- **Clarity First**: Documents must be actionable and easy to understand
- **Efficiency**: Leverage templates, AI, and systems to reduce repetitive work
- **Customer Empathy**: Understand the user's need to save time
- **Continuous Improvement**: Regularly update packs based on feedback
- **Remote-Friendly**: All roles can be remote with async communication

### Legal & Administrative
- Contractors vs employees: Start with contractors to test fit
- Clear IP agreements: All work product belongs to KG Services
- Confidentiality: Protect pack contents and business methods
- Compliance: Follow South African labor laws if hiring employees
- Payroll: Use simple payroll service or contractor invoicing
