# Marketing & Go-to-Market Model for KG Services

## 1. Marketing Motion Overview
KG Services employs a marketing-led growth model combining targeted campaigns, agency-style retainers, and product promotional drives to acquire customers and drive revenue.

## 2. Target Audience & Segmentation
- Primary: CMO, VP of Marketing, Head of Growth, CEO at mid-market B2B ($10M–$100M revenue)
- Secondary: Enterprise innovation teams, early-stage founders
- Segmentation by product:
  - KG ProcessPro: Finance Controllers, Accounts Payable Managers, CFOs
  - KG InsightFlow: VP of Marketing, Director of Marketing Analytics, Marketing Managers
  - KG ConnectHub: Enterprise Innovation Teams, Startup Founders, Product Managers, API Developers

## 3. Demand Generation Tactics
### 3.1 Campaigns
- Quarterly thematic campaigns aligned with product launches
- ABM (Account-Based Marketing) for top 50 target accounts per product
- LinkedIn Sponsored Content & InMail targeting job titles and industries
- Google Search Ads for high-intent keywords (e.g., "invoice automation software", "marketing ROI platform")
- Retargeting via LinkedIn and Google Display Network

### 3.2 Content Marketing
- Monthly blog posts addressing pain points and product benefits
- Bi-weekly case studies and customer success stories
- Quarterly whitepapers/eBooks (e.g., "The State of AP Automation 2027")
- Webinar series: monthly live demos + Q&A
- SEO-optimized landing pages per product

### 3.3 Agency-Style Retainers
- Offer retained marketing services to customers needing ongoing campaign management
- Scope includes content creation, ad management, lead nurturing
- Priced as % of ad spend or fixed monthly fee

### 3.4 Partnerships & Alliances
- Technology partnerships with ERP/accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero, SAP) for co-marketing
- Agency partnerships for implementation services
- Industry associations and events for thought leadership

## 4. Pricing Strategy
- Transparent subscription tiers as defined in Product Portfolio
- Freemium or free trial not offered; instead, provide pilot programs (3-month, discounted)
- Volume discounts for multi-product contracts
- Annual prepayment discount (10%)
- Discount for early adopters (first 10 customers)

## 5. Promotional Strategy
- Launch discounts for GA releases
- Referral program: existing customers receive 1 month free for each new signed contract
- Customer advocacy program: case studies, references, speaking opportunities
- Promotional bundles (e.g., ProcessPro + InsightFlow at bundled rate)

## 6. Channel Strategy
### 6.1 Direct Sales
- Inside sales team for SMB/mid-market
- Field sales for enterprise accounts
- Sales enablement: battle cards, ROI calculators, objection handling scripts

### 6.2 Partners & Agencies
- Referral partners: pay 10% of first-year ACV for qualified leads
- Reseller partners: white-label options for regional distributors
- Implementation partners: certified consultants for complex integrations

### 6.3 Online Self-Service
- Website with clear product pages, pricing, and demo request
- Automated email nurture sequences post-demo
- Chatbot for initial qualification and scheduling

## 7. Metrics & ROI Measurement
### 7.1 Leading Indicators
- MQLs per campaign
- Cost per Lead (CPL)
- Marketing Qualified Account (MQA) rate
- Website engagement (time on page, content downloads)

### 7.2 Lagging Indicators
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Payback Period (target <12 months)
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- LTV:CAC ratio (target >3:1)
- Conversion rates: MQL→SQL, SQL→Opportunity, Opportunity→Closed-Won

### 7.3 Reporting
- Weekly marketing performance dashboard
- Monthly executive summary
- Quarterly business review (QBR) with board

## 8. Budget Allocation Guidelines
- Allocate 40% to demand generation (ads, content creation)
- 30% to marketing operations (tools, personnel)
- 20% to events and partnerships
- 10% to experimentation and emerging channels
- Review and adjust quarterly based on ROI

## 9. Sales & Marketing Alignment
- Service Level Agreement (SLA): Marketing delivers X MQLs per week; Sales follows up within 24 hours
- Weekly sync meetings between marketing and sales leads
- Shared CRM (HubSpot/Salesforce) with closed-loop reporting
- Joint planning of campaigns and product launches
