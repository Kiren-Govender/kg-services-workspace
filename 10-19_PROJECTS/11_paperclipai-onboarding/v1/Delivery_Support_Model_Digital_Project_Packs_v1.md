# KG Services Delivery & Support Model
## For Digital Project Packs Business

### Delivery Model Overview
We deliver digital project packs instantly via Systeme.io upon purchase.
There are no onboarding calls, implementation services, or SLAs.

### Delivery Process
1. **Purchase Completion**:
   - Customer completes payment via Systeme.io checkout (credit card/PayPal)
   - Payment gateway confirms transaction

2. **Instant Access Grant**:
   - Systeme.io automatically grants access to the purchased course module
   - Customer receives email with login/link to access course

3. **Course Module Access**:
   - Customer logs into Systeme.io member area
   - Sees course titled with project pack name
   - Course contains:
     - Download button for ZIP package (all 9 files)
     - Video lessons section (MP4 files, if applicable)
     - Text-based user guide (04-User-Guide.docx viewable online)
     - Any additional resources (AI prompts, etc.)

4. **File Download**:
   - Customer clicks download button
   - Receives ZIP file named according to project pack (e.g., "Non-Profit-Fundraiser-Pack.zip")
   - ZIP contains the 9 core files:
     * 01-Introduction.docx
     * 02-Proposal.docx
     * 03-Checklist.xlsx (REQUIRED)
     * 04-User-Guide.docx
     * 05-Video-Script.docx (internal reference)
     * 06-AI-Prompt.txt
     * 07-Core-Deliverable (varies by project)
     * 08-Supporting-Asset
     * 09-Reference-Asset

5. **Post-Delivery**:
   - Customer extracts ZIP and uses documents immediately
   - No further action required from KG Services for basic delivery

### Support Model Overview
Support is email-only via Systeme.io, supplemented by a knowledge base in Notion.
We do NOT provide onboarding calls, implementation assistance, SLAs, or dedicated CSMs.

### Support Process
1. **Customer Initiates Contact**:
   - Customer replies to Systeme.io purchase email
   - Or uses support link in Systeme.io footer/member area
   - Or emails support@kgservices.co.za (if configured)

2. **Ticket Creation**:
   - Systeme.io automatically creates support ticket
   - Or email forwarded to support system (we can use Systeme.io's built-in support or connect to Help Scout/Zendesk later)

3. **Response Protocol**:
   - Target response time: Within 24 business hours
   - Responses handled by designated support person (part-time or founder initially)
   - Common issues addressed:
     * Download problems (ZIP corruption, blocked by antivirus)
     * File format questions (Which program to open .docx/.xlsx)
     * Content clarification (How to use a specific template)
     * Missing files (if ZIP incomplete)
     * Video playback issues (if applicable)

4. **Knowledge Base**:
   - Maintained in Notion (public or private shared)
   - Contains:
     * FAQ: Download and extraction instructions
     * FAQ: Software requirements (Microsoft Office, Google Docs, etc.)
     * How-to guides for common project types
     * Video tutorial links (if available)
     * Contact information and support hours
   - Updated based on recurring support questions

5. **Escalation Path**:
   - If support cannot resolve issue (e.g., content error, missing file):
     * Alert content/design team
     * Fix applied and updated ZIP uploaded to Systeme.io
     * Customer notified of fix and offered re-download
   - Refunds processed only if issue cannot be resolved and customer insists
     * Refund policy: 30-day money-back guarantee (if advertised) or case-by-case

### What We Do NOT Provide
- ✘ Onboarding or setup calls
- ✘ Implementation services or consulting
- ✘ Customization of documents for specific clients
- ✘ Training workshops or webinars (beyond included video lessons)
- ✘ Dedicated account managers or CSMs
- ✘ Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for response/resolution time
- ✘ Phone support (email only)
- ✘ In-person assistance or site visits

### Tools for Delivery & Support
- **Primary**: Systeme.io (delivery, basic email support)
- **Knowledge Base**: Notion (internal/external FAQ)
- **Email**: Systeme.io email or custom domain email (support@)
- **File Hosting**: Systeme.io (ZIP storage and delivery)
- **Optional**: Help Scout/Zendesk (if volume justifies separate ticket system)
- **Optional**: Loom/Screenrec (for creating video walkthroughs)

### Metrics to Monitor
- **Delivery Success Rate**: % of purchases with successful download (target >99%)
- **Support Volume**: Tickets per 100 sales (target <5)
- **First Response Time**: Average hours to first reply (target <24)
- **Resolution Time**: Average hours to close ticket (target <48)
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Post-support survey rating (target 4.5/5)
- **Refund Rate**: % of sales refunded (target <2%)

### Continuous Improvement
- Monthly review of support tickets to identify:
  * Common download issues → improve instructions
  * Frequent content questions → enhance user guide
  * Requested new files → consider for pack updates
- Quarterly update of knowledge base based on top ticket categories
- Annual review of delivery process for friction points
