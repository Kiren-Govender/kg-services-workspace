### Cost Structure
#### Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) - Digital Delivery
- Systeme.io platform fee: $27/month (unlimited plan) or 2% transaction fee (we'll assume fixed)
- Transaction fees (payment processors): ~2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
- Average transaction value: ~$100 (weighted average)
- Estimated COGS per sale: $3.20-$5.00
- **COGS % of Revenue**: ~3-5% (very low for digital products)

#### Operating Expenses
1. **Content Creation** (Primary Cost):
   - Content creators (contract or part-time): $15-$25/hour
   - Average time to create one pack: 8-12 hours
   - Cost per pack: $120-$300
   - With 9 files per pack, amortized over expected sales

2. **Design & Formatting**:
   - Designer (contract): $20-$35/hour
   - Time per pack: 2-3 hours
   - Cost per pack: $40-$105

3. **Marketing**:
   - Advertising budget: Start with 10-15% of revenue
   - Content distribution: Minimal (organic focus)
   - Email marketing: Included in Systeme.io

4. **Platform & Tools**:
   - Systeme.io: $27/month (unlimited) or transaction-based
   - Notion (knowledge base): $5-$10/user/month
   - Design tools (Canva Pro): $12.99/user/month
   - Miscellaneous: $50/month

5. **Customer Support**:
   - Email support (part-time): $15/hour
   - Estimated 5-10 minutes per customer
   - Low volume due to self-serve nature

6. **Administrative**:
   - Accounting, legal, etc.: Minimal initially

### Profitability Metrics
- **Gross Margin**: 95-97% (after transaction fees)
- **Contribution Margin**: 80-85% (after content/design amortization)
- **Break-even Analysis**:
  - Fixed costs (tools, platform): ~$100/month
  - Variable cost per sale: ~$5
  - Break-even volume: Very low (<20 sales/month at $64 average price)

### Cash Flow Considerations
- **Upfront Investment**: Content creation for initial library
- **Revenue Recognition**: Immediate upon purchase (no deferred revenue)
- **Payment Processing**: Daily/weekly deposits
- **Low Working Capital Requirements**: No inventory, minimal accounts receivable

### Key Financial Ratios (Target)
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: <$20 (via organic/content marketing)
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $64 (individual) to $349 (bundles)
- **Purchase Frequency**: Primarily one-time, with upsell/bundle opportunities
- **Refund Rate**: Target <2% (digital delivery, clear expectations)

### Sensitivity Analysis
- **Price Elasticity**: Test different price points within ranges
- **Volume Scaling**: As library grows, cross-sell and bundle opportunities increase
- **Seasonality**: Adjust marketing around business planning seasons (Q1, Q3)

### Investment Requirements (Initial)
- Content creation for 20 starter packs: 20 packs × 10 hours × $20/hour = $4,000
- Design for 20 packs: 20 packs × 2.5 hours × $25/hour = $1,250
- Marketing launch budget: $500 (ads, promotions)
- Tools setup: Minimal (existing subscriptions)
- **Total Initial Investment**: ~$6,000 (can be phased)

### Long-Term Financial Outlook
- **Year 1**: Focus on library building (20-40 packs) and market testing
- **Year 2**: Scale to 80-100 packs, introduce bundles, optimize funnels
- **Year 3**: Full library (38-39 packs), multiple bundles, potential licensing
- **Revenue Growth**: Primarily from volume and average order value increases via bundles
