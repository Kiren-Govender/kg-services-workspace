# Tool Stack for Digital Project Packs Business

## Primary Platform
- **Systeme.io**: All-in-one marketing platform for landing pages, email automation, course delivery, and affiliate management.
  - Funnel creation (landing pages, thank you pages)
  - Email sequences and broadcasts
  - Course hosting for video lessons
  - Digital product delivery (ZIP packs)
  - Payment processing (one-time purchases, bundles)
  - Affiliate program management
  - Blog and SEO capabilities

## Content Creation Workflow
### Document Creation
- **Microsoft Office 365 / Google Workspace**: For creating .docx, .xlsx, .pdf files
- **LibreOffice**: Free alternative for document creation
- **Adobe Acrobat Pro**: For PDF editing and form creation (if needed)
- **Canva**: For designing visual assets, covers, and supplemental graphics

### Video Production
- **Smartphone / Webcam**: For recording video lessons
- **OBS Studio**: Free screen recording and video capture software
- **DaVinci Resolve / Adobe Premiere Pro**: Video editing (free/paid options)
- **Audacity**: Audio editing and cleanup
- **Descript**: Optional for transcription and video editing via text

### AI Assistance
- **OpenAI API / Anthropic Claude**: For generating AI prompts, content outlines, and draft text
- **Notion AI**: For organizing content and generating templates
- **Grammarly**: For proofreading and grammar checking

## Project Management & Organization
- **Notion**: Central workspace for:
  - Project pack inventory and tracking
  - Content creation pipeline (Kanban boards)
  - Standard operating procedures (SOPs)
  - Knowledge base for customer support
  - Marketing calendar and campaign tracking
- **Trello / Asana**: Alternative for task management if preferred

## File Storage & Delivery
- **Systeme.io File Storage**: Hosts all digital product files (ZIP packs, course videos)
- **Google Drive / Dropbox**: Backup storage and collaborative workspace
- **WeTransfer / File.io**: For sending large files if needed (rare)

## Email & Communication
- **Systeme.io Email Automation**: Primary email sequences and broadcasts
- **Gmail / Outlook**: Direct customer support and personal communication
- **Zoom**: Optional for occasional customer calls or training (not core)

## Analytics & Tracking
- **Systeme.io Analytics**: Built-in funnel and sales tracking
- **Google Analytics**: For blog and landing page performance (if using custom domain)
- **Facebook Pixel**: For retargeting ads (if running paid ads)
- **Notion Dashboards**: Custom revenue and metric tracking

## Security & Legal
- **Terms of Service Generator**: For creating legal terms (e.g., Termly.io)
- **Privacy Policy Generator**: For GDPR/POPIA compliance
- **PayPal / Stripe**: Integrated via Systeme.io for payments (no separate handling needed)
- **Canva Pro**: For licensed graphics and templates (optional)

## Workflow Summary
1. **Content Creation**: Create documents, spreadsheets, and video scripts in Office/Google Suite.
2. **Asset Production**: Record and edit video lessons using smartphone/OBS and editing software.
3. **AI Assistance**: Use AI prompts to generate outlines, drafts, and variations.
4. **Assembly**: Compile 9 files per pack (8 customer-facing + 1 internal video script) into ZIP.
5. **Upload to Systeme.io**: Create product listing, upload ZIP, set price, configure email delivery.
6. **Funnel Setup**: Create landing page, email sequence, and thank you page in Systeme.io.
7. **Drive Traffic**: Via blog posts, social media, free templates as lead magnets.
8. **Automated Delivery**: Systeme.io handles email delivery, ZIP download, and course access.
9. **Support**: Email via Systeme.io, knowledge base in Notion.

## Tools to Avoid (Per Guardrails)
- No custom software development tools (IDEs, GitHub, Docker, etc.) — we do not build SaaS.
- No server infrastructure, cloud computing (AWS, Azure) — we do not host custom platforms.
- No customer relationship management (CRM) systems beyond Systeme.io contacts.
- No complex marketing automation platforms — Systeme.io suffices for email sequences.
- No sales engagement tools — we rely on marketing-led self-serve funnel.

*This tool stack supports the digital project packs business model: content creation → pack packaging → Systeme.io funnel → email marketing.*
